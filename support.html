Greenroom Support

Help for wedding vendors using Greenroom

Questions, problems or ideas? Write to getgreenroomapp@gmail.com. We read every message and usually reply within two business days. If you can, tell us your phone model and what you were doing when it happened.

Common questions

I didn't get my 6-digit sign-in code.

Check your spam or junk folder, then tap Resend on the code screen. Codes arrive within a minute. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap Forgot password?, enter your email, type the 6-digit code we send, and choose a new password.

I'm not getting notifications.

Open your phone's Settings, then Notifications, then Greenroom, and make sure notifications are allowed. Inside Greenroom you can also turn moment reminders on or off for each wedding in Day settings.

The app says "No signal."

Greenroom keeps the last information it loaded so you can still read your timeline, and it sends your chat messages and check-ins once your connection returns. If it doesn't clear when you have signal, close the app fully and open it again.

Can I use Greenroom in Spanish?

Yes. Greenroom follows your phone's language, English or Spanish, and you can change it in the app.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: My events → Settings (gear) → Delete account. You can also write to us and we'll do it for you. See the Privacy Policy for what is removed.

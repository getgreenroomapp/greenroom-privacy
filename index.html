Greenroom Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Greenroom ("the app") is made by Blue Couch Productions, LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Contact us any time at getgreenroomapp@gmail.com.

What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, or your Apple or Google account email if you sign in with them.
  • Your profile: name, business name, role (photographer, DJ and so on), and, if you add them, your Instagram, phone number, website, email and a profile photo or logo.
  • Your language choice.

What you and your team add to a wedding

  • Wedding details: the couple's names, dates, places, the timeline and its notes and checklists.
  • Key people: names, photos and notes about family members and the wedding party, added by the planner or team so vendors can recognize them.
  • Notes and files (such as floor plans, seating charts and song lists).
  • Team chat and private messages, check-ins ("I'm here") and checklist ticks.
  • Your Day settings (such as reminders and dim mode).

From your phone

  • A notification address, so the app can send you notifications, and your phone's language for their wording.
  • Crash reports when the app breaks: the phone model, system version, app version and what went wrong. These don't include your name, email or messages.

We don't collect your location, contacts or browsing history, we don't show ads, and we don't sell or rent your information.

How we use it

Only to run Greenroom: to show each wedding to its team, keep everyone's phones up to date, send reminders and notifications, let teammates contact each other, and find and fix problems.

Who can see it

  • Your wedding teams. Everything on a wedding (including key people and files) is visible only to the vendors on that wedding's team. Private messages are visible only to the two people in them.
  • Other Greenroom users. Your name, business, role and photo can appear when someone searches for a vendor to add to a team. Your phone number shows on contact cards only if you turned that on in your profile.
  • Service providers that run parts of Greenroom for us, only as needed:
    • Supabase: stores the app's data and files (servers in the United States).
    • Expo, Apple and Google: deliver notifications, and Apple and Google handle their sign-in options.
    • Google Maps Platform: looks up places when you search for a location (the search text is sent to Google).
    • Brevo: sends sign-in code emails.
    • Sentry: receives crash reports.
  • The law. We may share information if required by law or to protect someone's safety.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can delete your account at any time in the app: My events → Settings (gear) → Delete account. This removes your profile, photo and your place on every wedding team. Weddings you created pass to a teammate who can edit them, or are deleted if there isn't one. Messages you sent stay visible to the team, shown as from a former member. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

You can also ask us to see, correct or delete your information by writing to getgreenroomapp@gmail.com.

Children

Greenroom is for wedding professionals and isn't directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and each wedding's information can be read only by its team. No system is perfectly secure, so please keep your sign-in email account safe.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
